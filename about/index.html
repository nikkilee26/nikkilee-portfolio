about me

hey there! i'm nikki.

I'm a recent Computer Science graduate from Williams College with a passion for art and designing thoughtful, user-centered experiences. I was drawn to UX/UI design because it combines creativity with intention. I'm always looking to grow as a designer, exploring ways to make interfaces intuitive, accessible, and enjoyable for every user.

I have over a year of hands-on UX/UI experience and currently serve as Lead UX/UI Designer at an early-stage startup, where I'm building a 0→1 platform to bridge differences through structured, facilitated online conversations.

Outside of work, I love dabbling in all things creative like painting, drawing, hip hop dancing, crocheting, and pottery making. Check out some of my art pieces below and how they have influenced my design decisions!
