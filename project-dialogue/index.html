Project Dialogue

Social Media Platform

Civic Tech AI Products 0→1 Startup

Societal Problem

Social platforms optimize for engagement over understanding, leaving little room for people with different viewpoints to connect meaningfully.

Product Challenge

How might we onboard users to a new kind of dialogue platform while teaching constructive conversation skills without overwhelming them before they experience its value?

Team

Founder
1 UI/UX Designer
3 Engineers

Role

Lead UI/UX Designer,
Advisory Board Member

Duration

Feb 2026 – Current

Key Outcome

I replaced a lengthy onboarding flow with an AI-powered practice conversation, reducing onboarding by ~50% while preserving the core goal of teaching constructive dialogue.

Product

A social platform for constructive conversations across different perspectives, built on intentional algorithms, dialogue literacy, and AI-assisted communication tools.

Common Weaknesses

  • Echo chambers
  • Outrage-driven engagement

  • No exposure to differing perspectives
  • No tools for productive dialogue

Matching Process

  • Optimizes for shared interests and retention
  • No support for navigating disagreement

Wireframes: From Complex to Focused

Our initial onboarding required:

  • Educational modules
  • 40+ question questionnaire
  • 3 guided conversations before reaching the platform

Too much friction before users could experience the platform's value —> guaranteed drop-off.

How might we introduce users to a completely new conversation platform and its unique interaction model without overwhelming them with lengthy explanations?

Before: High-Friction Onboarding

  • Educational modules upfront
  • 40+ questions before platform access
  • 3 matched conversations required
  • Risk: Drop-off before value was clear

After: Practice-First Onboarding

  • Short AI-guided practice conversation
  • Platform teaches through use, not explanation
  • Post-practice summary of communication strengths and areas to improve
  • Educational content available as optional in-platform support
  • Result: ~50% reduction in onboarding steps

Design Decisions in the Practice Screen

2-Minute Timer

Low-commitment, bounded — users know what they're signing up for.

Conversation Pulse

Real-time emotional tone, without interrupting the flow.

Guided Suggestions

Tips, not commands. Users accept or dismiss freely — supports their voice, not replaces it.

AI Practice Partner

Safe space to experience productive disagreement before live interactions.

Our Solution

A Better, Healthier Feed

AI-Guided Conversations

Live feedback to practice healthy debate and active listening.

Conversation Insights

Post-conversation feedback on communication strengths and growth areas.

Continuous Learning

In-app stories, lessons, and exercises to build dialogue skills.

Conversation Awareness

We designed three real-time indicators to help users understand how they communicate, not just what they say:

Difference Barometer

How different are your perspectives?

Collaboration Barometer

How collaboratively are you each engaging?

Conversation Trajectory

Is the discussion building understanding, or escalating tension?

Design Tradeoff: Feedback vs. Focus

Challenge: Live indicators add cognitive load to an already demanding interaction.

Decision: Made the barometers collapsible — users choose between continuous feedback or a distraction-free conversation. Balancing transparency with simplicity.

Designing AI Responsibly

  • AI supports users without speaking for them.
  • Feedback focuses on communication, not beliefs.
  • The goal is long-term skill building.

How We'll Measure Success

Reflection

What I Learned

I came into this project expecting the biggest challenge to be designing individual screens. Instead, it was designing an entirely new conversation experience. Every decision required balancing AI guidance with user autonomy, transparency with simplicity, and skill-building with a natural, engaging experience.

The biggest lesson: users shouldn't have to understand the entire system before experiencing its value. Replacing multiple onboarding steps with a single AI-guided practice conversation showed that we could preserve every onboarding goal while significantly reducing friction. Instead of telling users how the platform worked, we let them experience it firsthand.

This project showed me what design ownership really means: making decisions with incomplete information, balancing user and business needs, and turning a broad vision into something people can actually use and learn from.
