Sorcea Labs

Personalized Skincare & Market Intelligence Platform

Beauty Tech Startup B2B & B2C Market Intelligence

The Challenge

After rebranding from SearchOwl, Sorcea Labs needed to earn trust from two very different audiences at once; prospective brand partners who needed to understand the platform's value, and skincare shoppers who needed confidence to discover and buy the right products.

Team

3 Designers
1 Developer
2 Co-Founders

Role

UX/UI Design Intern

Duration

May – Aug 2025

Product

Sorcea Labs is a beauty-tech startup combining AI and skincare to help shoppers find personalized products while giving brands real-time market analytics and consumer insights.

Goal

Rebrand the Sorcea Labs B2B website to build credibility with brand partners, and redesign the Sorcea B2C platform to make skincare discovery more intuitive and confidence-driven for consumers.

Responsibilities

Competitive Analysis • Branding • Stakeholder Research • Wireframing • Design Iterations • High-Fidelity Mockups • Stakeholder Collaboration

define research ideation design solution impact reflection

01 Define

A dual design challenge: credibility for partners, confidence for shoppers.

Sorcea Labs operates across two distinct user groups with entirely different needs. Designing for both required separate strategies grounded in the same core goal — building trust.

Sorcea Labs

B2B: Unclear brand identity post-rebrand

After rebranding from SearchOwl, the business site failed to communicate what Sorcea Labs did, why it was different, or why brands should partner with it.

Sorcea

B2C: Shoppers lacked confidence in product choices

Skincare consumers felt uncertain about which products would work for them, leading to abandoned searches, endless scrolling, and low purchase confidence.

Primary objectives

Two websites. Two audiences. One design direction.

Based on these challenges, my role focused on two design goals that had to feel cohesive while speaking to entirely different people:

  • Business Website (Sorcea Labs) — How might we develop a sophisticated, tech-forward identity that communicates value to brands and makes Sorcea Labs feel like a credible, innovative partner?
  • Skincare Platform (Sorcea) — How might we streamline the shopping experience so users feel guided toward products that actually match their skin's needs?

02 Research

What competitors revealed about building trust.

Through conversations with the co-founders, I understood that Sorcea Labs needed to merge beauty and tech aesthetics, while Sorcea required a beauty-focused, consumer-centered feel. I conducted a SWOT analysis of competitors across both industries to understand what made a site feel "tech-forward" vs. "beauty-driven" and what trust signals each context required.

Insight

Tech B2B sites that perform well lead with a bold, specific value proposition — not a generic tagline — supported by proof points like case studies or partner logos.

→

Design Decision

Lead the Sorcea Labs hero with a strong, memorable tagline ("Unlock the future of beauty") and scannable cards that highlight key differentiators.

Insight

Beauty platforms that convert well rely on dynamic imagery, personalized search, and minimal friction between intent and product discovery.

→

Design Decision

Bring filters inline with the search bar so users can refine their query in one interaction instead of navigating separate dropdowns or pop-ups.

Insight

Competitor beauty sites often buried personalization behind quizzes or optional extras, treating it as a feature rather than the core experience.

→

Design Decision

Make personalization the front door of Sorcea — dynamic imagery, AI-assisted search, and editable filters communicate that the platform adapts to you.

03 Ideation

Design principles to guide both sites.

From competitive research and founder conversations, I distilled a set of principles to anchor design decisions across both the business site and the skincare platform. Each principle directly addressed a gap identified in the research.

Sorcea Labs

Signal Credibility Fast

Partners make quick judgments. Lead with proof — clear differentiators, consistent brand identity, and a hero section that communicates value immediately.

Lead With Impact

A bold, specific tagline and strong hero visuals should capture attention and communicate what makes Sorcea Labs different before the user scrolls.

Create a Modern Feel

Interactive, dynamic elements position Sorcea Labs as a tech-forward company — not just another analytics dashboard.

Sorcea

Personalization as the Default

Treat personalization as the core experience, not an add-on — guide users toward products that match their specific skin concerns from the first interaction.

Credibility & Trust

A strong first impression built on clear brand values, high-quality visuals, and social proof helps shoppers feel confident in the platform before they even search.

Skincare Aesthetic

Clean neutral tones, high-quality product imagery, and a calm visual feel signal that Sorcea understands the beauty category and earns the shopper's trust.

04 Design

Iterating toward clarity on both sides.

Working collaboratively with two other designers, we each explored independent directions and came together regularly to share progress, combine the strongest ideas, and pressure-test decisions against the design principles. The final designs synthesized work from the full team.

Sorcea Labs landing page evolution

Three iterations refined the business site from a busy, unclear direction into a sophisticated, tech-forward identity that communicates Sorcea Labs' value clearly to prospective partners.

Iteration 1

  • Email capture was direct and clear.
  • Moving background felt distracting and hard to read.
  • Visuals felt busy and did not communicate the brand difference.

Iteration 2

  • "Unlock the future of beauty" became a stronger, more memorable tagline — sets the brand apart immediately.
  • Hero image aligned better with the tech direction.
  • Additional sections advertised the company more clearly.

Iteration 3 ★

  • Background color and type felt more cohesive with the brand direction.
  • Header made it easier for brands to navigate to the Sorcea consumer platform.
  • Card-based selling points made the page easier to scan and understand quickly.

"Why Us" interaction

From a single large slideshow of questions to smaller cycling bubbles — more playful, easier to scan, and interactive on scroll.

Sorcea platform exploration

For Sorcea's landing page, I aimed to present all key information on a single, easy-to-navigate page. I also refined the design while preserving Sorcea's natural language search and filters to help users find personalized products with less friction.

Moving filters inline with the search bar reduced extra steps and made the experience feel more editable and responsive to the user's intent.

Design 1

Filters locked behind a pop-up — inconvenient and created unnecessary friction in the search flow.

Design 2

Two separate dropdowns added cognitive load — users had to remember their selections across interactions.

Design 3 ★

Filters integrated directly into the search bar — instantly editable, one place, no extra steps.

Favorites page

05 Solution

Final direction.

Sorcea Labs

Reintroducing SearchOwl as a credible tech partner.

  • Sophisticated, tech-forward aesthetic to signal expertise to brand partners
  • Clear and consistent brand identity that eliminated post-rebrand confusion
  • Scannable selling points structured so partners can understand the value in seconds
  • Compelling hero section with a bold tagline and visuals that communicate the brand difference immediately
  • Dynamic, interactive interface that signals innovation and builds confidence in the product

Sorcea

Crafting Sorcea's bold, personalized experience.

  • Eye-catching, dynamic front cover that draws shoppers in before they even start searching
  • Memorable tagline that communicates the platform's core promise
  • Personalized, user-centered discovery with inline filters that adapt to each shopper's needs

For Brand Partners

  • Time spent on the Sorcea Labs business site

For Shoppers

  • Product conversion rate from search to purchase

07 Reflection

What I learned.

Sorcea Labs taught me how to translate market research, founder priorities, and consumer needs into a cohesive brand and product direction, even when those audiences wanted very different things.

The most challenging part was designing for two different trust signals at once. The B2B site needed to feel confident and tech-forward, while the consumer platform needed to feel warm, personalized, and easy. Maintaining both directions without compromising the other required clear principles and deliberate visual choices.

I also learned how valuable it is to share half-baked ideas early. Some of the decisions that ended up in the final product came from rough explorations I almost didn't show. This made me realize that collaborative design depends on the willingness to think out loud, not just present finished work.
